    Study Spot Generator: Term project from Intro to Software Engineering (CS 320).
    Team: Kevin Luo, Mara Oancea, and Nishani Clarke:
  • Problem: Students at Brown often find it difficult/time-consuming to find a study spot that meets their needs
  • Our solution: A Study Spot Generator where a user enters their preferences (e.g., noise level, walking distance, atmosphere), and gets matched with an optimized study spot recommendation, ensuring high satisfaction.
  • My role: Implemented backend matching algorithm, implemented API calls using IPStack to access user location, utilized Firestore to store user preferences and favorite study spots between consecutive logins, and integrated Firestore and Clerk to build a secure login system and ensure personalized experiences.
  • My process: I began by analyzing how users make decisions about study spaces, translating these preferences into concrete parameters that could be used algorithmically. I designed the backend to balance logic and nuance—using conditionally weighted matching to interpret inputs like "atmosphere" or "noise level". I used Firestore to structure persistent user data, enabling learning over time (e.g., remembering favorites or adapting to frequent choices). As someone passionate about the intersection of intelligence and creative spirit, I loved experimenting with how we could encode personality and vibe into data—abstracting subjective experiences into a form that still respected the messiness of human preference. Debugging and optimizing API integration also challenged me to think flexibly across layers of the stack, ensuring fast, personalized, and secure recommendations every time a user logs in.

    Redesign of Seed Cafe’s Webpage

My process: I began by identifying usability and branding issues on the original Seed Cafe site, then created a consistent Figma prototype with cohesive colors, typography, and layout. I restructured key content like hours and menu for better visibility, enhanced the cafe’s online identity with custom visuals, and emphasized improvement of visual hierarchy. By combining thoughtful visual design with accessibility principles, I created a site that’s not only more intuitive and inclusive, but also reflects the cafe’s unique identity with clarity and charm. I ensured the website was accessible by testing for color contrast, tab navigation, and responsiveness on three different screen sizes.
